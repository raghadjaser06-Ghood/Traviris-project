Mountains & Desert

Jordan’s Hidden Places

Burdah Rock Bridge

One of the highest natural rock bridges in Wadi Rum, reached by a steep scramble with amazing desert views.

Climb Burdah Arch

Scramble up the red rocks with a Bedouin guide, stand on top of the natural bridge, and look over the endless Wadi Rum desert.

  • Location: Eastern side of Wadi Rum Protected Area.
  • Price: Wadi Rum entry 5 JOD + guide and jeep from about 40 JOD.
  • Activities: Rock scrambling, hiking, and photography.
  • Services: Bedouin guides, jeep tours, and camps nearby.

Jabal Umm ad Dami

Jordan's highest mountain, a quiet peak near the Saudi border with views all the way to the Red Sea on clear days.

Summit Jordan's Highest Peak

Hike to the top of Jordan's highest mountain, watch the sunset over the desert, and spend the night under the stars.

  • Location: Southern Wadi Rum, near the Saudi border.
  • Price: Guided trips from about 50 JOD per person.
  • Activities: Mountain hiking, sunset watching, and stargazing.
  • Services: Bedouin guides and desert camps.

Most Famous Spots

Wadi Rum

The famous red desert of Jordan, known for its huge mountains, sand dunes, and Bedouin camps.

Jeep Safari & Bedouin Camp

Ride a jeep across the red sand, visit old rock drawings, and spend the night in a Bedouin camp under a sky full of stars.

  • Location: Wadi Rum, about 1 hour north of Aqaba.
  • Price: Entry 5 JOD, jeep tours from about 30 JOD.
  • Activities: Jeep tours, camel rides, camping, and stargazing.
  • Services: Bedouin camps, bubble tents, and traditional zarb dinner.

Mount Nebo

A holy mountain with wide views over the Jordan Valley and the Dead Sea.

Panoramic Viewpoint

Stand on top of the mountain, see the Jordan Valley and the Dead Sea below, and visit the old church with its beautiful mosaics.

  • Location: Near Madaba, about 45 minutes from Amman.
  • Price: About 3 JOD per person.
  • Activities: Sightseeing, mosaics, and sunset views.
  • Services: Visitor center, café, and souvenir shops.
