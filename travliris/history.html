History & Heritage

Jordan’s Hidden Places

Pella (Tabaqat Fahl)

A quiet ancient city in the green Jordan Valley, with ruins from thousands of years of history.

Ancient City in the Valley

Walk between Roman columns, old Byzantine churches, and a small Roman theater, then enjoy a view over the Jordan Valley from the hill.

  • Location: Northern Jordan Valley, near Irbid.
  • Price: Free or a small entry fee (check before you go).
  • Activities: Exploring ruins, hiking, and photography.
  • Services: Local guides and a rest house with valley views.

Qasr al-Abd

A beautiful old palace in a green valley west of Amman, with lion carvings on its walls.

Ancient Palace & Caves

Visit the old palace with its animal carvings, explore the caves nearby, and shop at the local women's handicraft center.

  • Location: Iraq al-Amir, Wadi Al Seer, 30 minutes from Amman.
  • Price: About 3 JOD per person.
  • Activities: Exploring the palace and caves, handicraft shopping.
  • Services: Women's handicraft cooperative and café.

Most Famous Spots

Petra

The Rose City, carved into pink rock, and one of the New Seven Wonders of the World.

Walk the Siq to the Treasury

Walk through the narrow Siq to the Treasury, climb up to the Monastery, and come back at night for Petra by Night.

  • Location: Wadi Musa, about 3 hours south of Amman.
  • Price: 50 JOD for one day, free with the Jordan Pass.
  • Activities: Hiking, sightseeing, and Petra by Night.
  • Services: Guides, horses and carriages, and restaurants.

Jerash

One of the best-kept Roman cities in the world, with long columned streets and theaters.

Roman City Tour

Walk the long columned street, stand in the Oval Plaza, and watch the Roman army and chariot show.

  • Location: Jerash, about 50 minutes north of Amman.
  • Price: About 10 JOD, free with the Jordan Pass.
  • Activities: Sightseeing, history tours, and chariot show.
  • Services: Visitor center, guides, and restaurants.
